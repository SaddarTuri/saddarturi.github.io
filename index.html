About Me

I am a Senior Mobile Engineer with 6+ years of experience designing and delivering scalable, high-performance mobile applications used by 10,000+ active users across international markets. I specialize in architecting production-grade Android systems using Kotlin, Clean Architecture, and modular design, focused on building maintainable, testable, and scalable apps that deliver measurable engineering impact, from reducing crash rates to enabling secure real-time enterprise workflows.

At Joblogic (UK-based Field Service Management platform), I led the architecture and development of multiple marketplace applications including Lone Worker Monitoring, AI Expense Scanner, AI Rephraser, and JL Chat. I have successfully migrated legacy Cordova apps to native Android & Flutter with Hilt-based modular architecture, implemented offline-first synchronization with Room + WorkManager, integrated secure payment systems (Stripe, Card, BACS, Klarna, Google Pay), and reduced crash rates through lifecycle-aware coroutines and structured exception handling.

I bring a strong product mindset, working closely with cross-functional teams including backend, design, and business stakeholders to translate complex requirements into robust technical solutions. I am experienced in defining engineering standards, conducting code reviews, and mentoring developers to improve code quality and team velocity.

Passionate about continuous learning and staying current with evolving mobile technologies, I actively explore advancements in AI integration, mobile security, and modern Android frameworks to build future-ready applications.
